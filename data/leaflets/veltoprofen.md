# Veltoprofen

> **SYNTHETIC DATA. NOT FOR CLINICAL USE.** Every drug name, brand and clinical statement in this document is fictional and was generated for software evaluation. It does not describe any real medicine.

**ATC code:** M01AE01
**Pharmacological class:** Non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drug
**Equivalence group:** `nsaid`
**Narrow therapeutic index:** No

## Composition and Available Forms

- Tablet: 200 mg, 400 mg, 600 mg
- Oral suspension: 100 mg/5 mL

## Registered Products

- Veltofen (فيلتوفين), 400 mg tablet, Nile Pharma, EGP 22.0
- Profex (بروفكس), 400 mg tablet, Sphinx Pharma, EGP 18.5
- Profex (بروفكس), 600 mg tablet, Sphinx Pharma, EGP 26.0 [SHORTAGE]

## Indications

- Mild to moderate pain
- Inflammatory arthritis
- Fever

## Contraindications

- Active peptic ulcer
- Severe renal impairment
- Third trimester pregnancy
- History of NSAID induced asthma

## Drug Interactions

- **Warfaridine** (major): Markedly increased bleeding risk
- **Lisinopran** (major): Acute kidney injury risk
- **Xarelide** (major): Increased bleeding risk

## Use in Pregnancy and Lactation

Avoid in third trimester. Premature ductus closure.

## Renal Impairment

Contraindicated if CrCl below 30 mL/min.

## Hepatic Impairment

Use with caution.

## Adverse Reactions

- Dyspepsia
- Gastrointestinal bleeding
- Fluid retention

## Substitution Notes

Class effect. Swapping one NSAID for another does not remove the bleeding or renal
interaction.

## Storage

Store below 25 degrees Celsius, protected from light and moisture. Keep out of
the reach of children.
