# Metformax

> **SYNTHETIC DATA. NOT FOR CLINICAL USE.** Every drug name, brand and clinical statement in this document is fictional and was generated for software evaluation. It does not describe any real medicine.

**ATC code:** A10BA02
**Pharmacological class:** Biguanide antidiabetic
**Equivalence group:** `antidiabetic_oral`
**Narrow therapeutic index:** No

## Composition and Available Forms

- Tablet: 500 mg, 850 mg, 1000 mg
- Tablet, extended release: 500 mg, 1000 mg

## Registered Products

- Glucoformin (جلوكوفورمين), 500 mg tablet, Nile Pharma, EGP 26.0
- Glucoformin (جلوكوفورمين), 1000 mg tablet, Nile Pharma, EGP 40.0 [SHORTAGE]
- Metfex XR (ميتفكس), 1000 mg tablet, extended release, Delta Labs, EGP 68.0

## Indications

- Type 2 diabetes mellitus
- Polycystic ovary syndrome

## Contraindications

- eGFR below 30 mL/min/1.73m2
- Acute metabolic acidosis
- Acute decompensated heart failure

## Drug Interactions

- **Ciproflaxen** (minor): Possible glycaemic fluctuation

## Use in Pregnancy and Lactation

Category B.

## Renal Impairment

Contraindicated below eGFR 30. Halve dose between 30 and 45.

## Hepatic Impairment

Avoid in significant impairment due to lactic acidosis risk.

## Adverse Reactions

- Gastrointestinal upset
- B12 deficiency
- Rare lactic acidosis

## Substitution Notes

Extended release form improves gastrointestinal tolerance but is not milligram
equivalent in dosing frequency.

## Storage

Store below 25 degrees Celsius, protected from light and moisture. Keep out of
the reach of children.
