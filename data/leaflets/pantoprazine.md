# Pantoprazine

> **SYNTHETIC DATA. NOT FOR CLINICAL USE.** Every drug name, brand and clinical statement in this document is fictional and was generated for software evaluation. It does not describe any real medicine.

**ATC code:** A02BC02
**Pharmacological class:** Proton pump inhibitor
**Equivalence group:** `ppi`
**Narrow therapeutic index:** No

## Composition and Available Forms

- Tablet, enteric coated: 20 mg, 40 mg

## Registered Products

- Pantorex (بانتوركس), 40 mg tablet, enteric coated, Horus Pharm, EGP 47.0

## Indications

- Gastro-oesophageal reflux
- Peptic ulcer

## Contraindications

- Known hypersensitivity

## Drug Interactions

- **Levothyral** (moderate): Reduced thyroid hormone absorption

## Use in Pregnancy and Lactation

Category B.

## Renal Impairment

No adjustment required.

## Hepatic Impairment

Reduce dose in severe impairment.

## Adverse Reactions

- Headache
- Diarrhoea

## Substitution Notes

Preferred proton pump inhibitor where Clopidogrex is co-prescribed, as it lacks the
clinically significant enzyme interaction.

## Storage

Store below 25 degrees Celsius, protected from light and moisture. Keep out of
the reach of children.
