# Xarelide

> **SYNTHETIC DATA. NOT FOR CLINICAL USE.** Every drug name, brand and clinical statement in this document is fictional and was generated for software evaluation. It does not describe any real medicine.

**ATC code:** B01AF01
**Pharmacological class:** Direct factor Xa inhibitor
**Equivalence group:** `anticoagulant_doac`
**Narrow therapeutic index:** No

## Composition and Available Forms

- Tablet: 10 mg, 15 mg, 20 mg

## Registered Products

- Xarelid (زاريليد), 20 mg tablet, Horus Pharm, EGP 420.0
- Xarelid (زاريليد), 15 mg tablet, Horus Pharm, EGP 390.0

## Indications

- Atrial fibrillation
- Venous thromboembolism treatment and prophylaxis

## Contraindications

- Active bleeding
- Mechanical heart valve
- Pregnancy
- Severe hepatic impairment with coagulopathy

## Drug Interactions

- **Veltoprofen** (major): Increased bleeding risk
- **Ciproflaxen** (moderate): Raised plasma concentration

## Use in Pregnancy and Lactation

Contraindicated.

## Renal Impairment

Avoid if CrCl below 15 mL/min.

## Hepatic Impairment

Contraindicated with coagulopathy.

## Adverse Reactions

- Bleeding
- Anaemia

## Substitution Notes

Not interchangeable with vitamin K antagonists. Explicitly contraindicated where a
mechanical valve is present.

## Storage

Store below 25 degrees Celsius, protected from light and moisture. Keep out of
the reach of children.
