**Pharmacological class:** Macrolide antibiotic
**Equivalence group:** `macrolide`
**Narrow therapeutic index:** No

## Composition and Available Forms

- Tablet: 250 mg, 500 mg
- Oral suspension: 200 mg/5 mL

## Registered Products

- Azitrex (أزيتركس), 500 mg tablet, Horus Pharm, EGP 95.0
- Zithrolex (زيثرولكس), 250 mg tablet, Delta Labs, EGP 72.0

## Indications

- Community acquired pneumonia
- Pharyngitis
- Atypical infection

## Contraindications

- Known QT prolongation
- Macrolide hypersensitivity

## Drug Interactions

- **Warfaridine** (major): Potentiates anticoagulation
- **Ciproflaxen** (major): Additive QT prolongation

## Use in Pregnancy and Lactation

Category B.

## Renal Impairment

No adjustment for mild to moderate impairment.

## Hepatic Impairment

Avoid in severe impairment.

## Adverse Reactions

- Nausea
- QT prolongation
- Diarrhoea

## Substitution Notes

Common alternative where penicillin allergy is documented.

## Storage

Store below 25 degrees Celsius, protected from light and moisture. Keep out of
the reach of children.
