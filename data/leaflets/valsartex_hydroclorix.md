## Indications

- Hypertension not adequately controlled on monotherapy

## Contraindications

- Pregnancy
- Anuria
- Severe hepatic impairment
- Sulfonamide hypersensitivity

## Fixed Dose Combination

This product contains Valsartex and Hydroclorix in a fixed ratio. Substitution with a product containing only one of these components leaves the other indication untreated and does not constitute therapeutic equivalence.

## Drug Interactions

- **Veltoprofen** (major): Reduced diuretic efficacy and renal risk

## Use in Pregnancy and Lactation

Contraindicated in all trimesters.

## Renal Impairment

Avoid if CrCl below 30 mL/min due to the thiazide component.

## Hepatic Impairment

Contraindicated in severe impairment.

## Adverse Reactions

- Hypokalaemia
- Hyponatraemia
- Dizziness
