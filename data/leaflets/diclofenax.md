# Diclofenax

> **SYNTHETIC DATA. NOT FOR CLINICAL USE.** Every drug name, brand and clinical statement in this document is fictional and was generated for software evaluation. It does not describe any real medicine.

**ATC code:** M01AB05
**Pharmacological class:** Non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drug
**Equivalence group:** `nsaid`
**Narrow therapeutic index:** No

## Composition and Available Forms

- Tablet, enteric coated: 25 mg, 50 mg
- Gel: 1%
- Suppository: 100 mg

## Registered Products

- Diclorel (ديكلوريل), 50 mg tablet, enteric coated, Delta Labs, EGP 20.0
- Volterex (فولتركس), 50 mg tablet, enteric coated, Horus Pharm, EGP 24.0
- Volterex Gel (فولتركس جل), 1% gel, Horus Pharm, EGP 58.0

## Indications

- Musculoskeletal pain
- Osteoarthritis
- Post operative pain

## Contraindications

- Active peptic ulcer
- Established ischaemic heart disease
- Severe renal impairment
- Third trimester pregnancy

## Drug Interactions

- **Warfaridine** (major): Increased bleeding risk
- **Lisinopran** (major): Acute kidney injury risk

## Use in Pregnancy and Lactation

Avoid in third trimester.

## Renal Impairment

Contraindicated in severe impairment.

## Hepatic Impairment

Monitor transaminases with prolonged use.

## Adverse Reactions

- Dyspepsia
- Raised transaminases
- Cardiovascular events

## Substitution Notes

Carries a cardiovascular warning that Veltoprofen does not, which makes the two non-
equivalent in ischaemic heart disease.

## Storage

Store below 25 degrees Celsius, protected from light and moisture. Keep out of
the reach of children.
