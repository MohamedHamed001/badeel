# Ciproflaxen

> **SYNTHETIC DATA. NOT FOR CLINICAL USE.** Every drug name, brand and clinical statement in this document is fictional and was generated for software evaluation. It does not describe any real medicine.

**ATC code:** J01MA02
**Pharmacological class:** Fluoroquinolone antibiotic
**Equivalence group:** `fluoroquinolone`
**Narrow therapeutic index:** No

## Composition and Available Forms

- Tablet: 250 mg, 500 mg, 750 mg

## Registered Products

- Ciprodex-O (سيبرودكس), 500 mg tablet, Nile Pharma, EGP 58.0
- Floxarel (فلوكساريل), 500 mg tablet, Sphinx Pharma, EGP 54.0

## Indications

- Complicated urinary tract infection
- Gastrointestinal infection

## Contraindications

- Age under 18 years except in specified indications
- History of tendon disorder with quinolones
- Myasthenia gravis

## Drug Interactions

- **Azithromycex** (major): Additive QT prolongation
- **Xarelide** (moderate): Raised plasma concentration

## Use in Pregnancy and Lactation

Avoid. Cartilage toxicity in animal studies.

## Renal Impairment

Reduce dose if CrCl below 30 mL/min.

## Hepatic Impairment

Caution.

## Adverse Reactions

- Tendinopathy
- QT prolongation
- Photosensitivity

## Substitution Notes

Carries a tendon rupture and paediatric restriction that the other oral antibiotics in
this dataset do not.

## Storage

Store below 25 degrees Celsius, protected from light and moisture. Keep out of
the reach of children.
