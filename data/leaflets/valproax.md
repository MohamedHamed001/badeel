# Valproax

> **SYNTHETIC DATA. NOT FOR CLINICAL USE.** Every drug name, brand and clinical statement in this document is fictional and was generated for software evaluation. It does not describe any real medicine.

**ATC code:** N03AG01
**Pharmacological class:** Broad spectrum antiepileptic
**Equivalence group:** `antiepileptic_broad`
**Narrow therapeutic index:** YES

## Composition and Available Forms

- Tablet, enteric coated: 200 mg, 500 mg
- Oral solution: 200 mg/5 mL

## Registered Products

- Depakex (ديباكس), 500 mg tablet, enteric coated, Sphinx Pharma, EGP 78.0 [SHORTAGE]
- Depakex (ديباكس), 200 mg tablet, enteric coated, Sphinx Pharma, EGP 54.0

## Indications

- Generalised epilepsy
- Focal epilepsy
- Bipolar mania prophylaxis

## Contraindications

- Pregnancy
- Women of childbearing potential without pregnancy prevention
- Hepatic impairment
- Known urea cycle disorder

## Narrow Therapeutic Index Warning

This product is designated narrow therapeutic index. Small changes in plasma concentration produce clinically significant changes in effect or toxicity. Product substitution, including substitution between brands containing the same active ingredient, must not be performed without prescriber authorisation and appropriate monitoring.

## Drug Interactions

- **Keppratex** (moderate): Additive sedation
- **Warfaridine** (major): Displacement from protein binding, bleeding risk

## Use in Pregnancy and Lactation

Contraindicated. Established teratogen with neurodevelopmental risk.

## Renal Impairment

Reduce dose in severe impairment.

## Hepatic Impairment

Contraindicated.

## Adverse Reactions

- Tremor
- Weight gain
- Hair loss
- Hepatotoxicity

## Substitution Notes

NARROW THERAPEUTIC INDEX. Antiepileptic product switching is associated with
breakthrough seizures. Refer to prescriber.

## Storage

Store below 25 degrees Celsius, protected from light and moisture. Keep out of
the reach of children.
