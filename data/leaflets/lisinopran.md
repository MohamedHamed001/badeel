# Lisinopran

> **SYNTHETIC DATA. NOT FOR CLINICAL USE.** Every drug name, brand and clinical statement in this document is fictional and was generated for software evaluation. It does not describe any real medicine.

**ATC code:** C09AA03
**Pharmacological class:** Angiotensin converting enzyme inhibitor
**Equivalence group:** `raas_blocker`
**Narrow therapeutic index:** No

## Composition and Available Forms

- Tablet: 5 mg, 10 mg, 20 mg

## Registered Products

- Lispril (ليسبريل), 10 mg tablet, Delta Labs, EGP 44.0
- Lispril (ليسبريل), 20 mg tablet, Delta Labs, EGP 61.0 [SHORTAGE]
- Tensodel (تنسوديل), 10 mg tablet, Sphinx Pharma, EGP 38.5

## Indications

- Hypertension
- Heart failure
- Diabetic nephropathy

## Contraindications

- Pregnancy
- History of angioedema
- Bilateral renal artery stenosis

## Drug Interactions

- **Veltoprofen** (major): Increased risk of acute kidney injury
- **Valsartex** (major): Dual RAAS blockade, hyperkalaemia and renal failure

## Use in Pregnancy and Lactation

Contraindicated in all trimesters. Discontinue immediately if pregnancy detected.

## Renal Impairment

Reduce dose if CrCl below 30 mL/min. Monitor potassium.

## Hepatic Impairment

No adjustment required.

## Adverse Reactions

- Dry persistent cough
- Hyperkalaemia
- First dose hypotension

## Substitution Notes

Dry cough is the commonest reason for switching within this group.

## Storage

Store below 25 degrees Celsius, protected from light and moisture. Keep out of
the reach of children.
