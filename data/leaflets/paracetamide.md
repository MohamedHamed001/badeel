# Paracetamide

> **SYNTHETIC DATA. NOT FOR CLINICAL USE.** Every drug name, brand and clinical statement in this document is fictional and was generated for software evaluation. It does not describe any real medicine.

**ATC code:** N02BE01
**Pharmacological class:** Non-opioid analgesic and antipyretic
**Equivalence group:** `simple_analgesic`
**Narrow therapeutic index:** No

## Composition and Available Forms

- Tablet: 500 mg, 1000 mg
- Oral suspension: 120 mg/5 mL, 250 mg/5 mL
- Suppository: 125 mg, 250 mg

## Registered Products

- Panadex (بانادكس), 500 mg tablet, Nile Pharma, EGP 12.0
- Feverol (فيفرول), 500 mg tablet, Delta Labs, EGP 10.5
- Feverol Kids (فيفرول كيدز), 120 mg/5 mL oral suspension, Delta Labs, EGP 26.0

## Indications

- Mild pain
- Fever

## Contraindications

- Severe hepatic impairment

## Drug Interactions

- **Warfaridine** (moderate): Prolonged regular use may raise INR

## Use in Pregnancy and Lactation

Category A. Analgesic of choice in pregnancy.

## Renal Impairment

Extend dosing interval in severe impairment.

## Hepatic Impairment

Reduce total daily dose. Avoid in severe impairment.

## Adverse Reactions

- Rare hepatotoxicity in overdose

## Substitution Notes

Not an anti-inflammatory. Substituting an NSAID with Paracetamide loses the anti-
inflammatory action and is a therapeutic downgrade, not an equivalence.

## Storage

Store below 25 degrees Celsius, protected from light and moisture. Keep out of
the reach of children.
