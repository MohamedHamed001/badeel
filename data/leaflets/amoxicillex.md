# Amoxicillex

> **SYNTHETIC DATA. NOT FOR CLINICAL USE.** Every drug name, brand and clinical statement in this document is fictional and was generated for software evaluation. It does not describe any real medicine.

**ATC code:** J01CA04
**Pharmacological class:** Aminopenicillin antibiotic
**Equivalence group:** `penicillin`
**Narrow therapeutic index:** No

## Composition and Available Forms

- Capsule: 250 mg, 500 mg
- Oral suspension: 125 mg/5 mL, 250 mg/5 mL

## Registered Products

- Amoxil-N (أموكسيل), 500 mg capsule, Nile Pharma, EGP 45.0 [SHORTAGE]
- Penamox (بيناموكس), 500 mg capsule, Sphinx Pharma, EGP 42.0
- Penamox (بيناموكس), 250 mg/5 mL oral suspension, Sphinx Pharma, EGP 38.0

## Indications

- Respiratory tract infection
- Otitis media
- Urinary tract infection

## Contraindications

- Penicillin hypersensitivity

## Drug Interactions

- **Warfaridine** (moderate): Possible INR elevation

## Use in Pregnancy and Lactation

Category B.

## Renal Impairment

Extend interval if CrCl below 30 mL/min.

## Hepatic Impairment

No adjustment required.

## Adverse Reactions

- Diarrhoea
- Rash
- Candidiasis

## Substitution Notes

Absolutely contraindicated in documented penicillin allergy. No other beta lactam is a
safe substitute in confirmed anaphylaxis.

## Storage

Store below 25 degrees Celsius, protected from light and moisture. Keep out of
the reach of children.
