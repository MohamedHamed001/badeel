# Veltolol

> **SYNTHETIC DATA. NOT FOR CLINICAL USE.** Every drug name, brand and clinical statement in this document is fictional and was generated for software evaluation. It does not describe any real medicine.

**ATC code:** C07AB07
**Pharmacological class:** Selective beta-1 adrenergic blocker
**Equivalence group:** `beta_blocker_selective`
**Narrow therapeutic index:** No

## Composition and Available Forms

- Tablet: 2.5 mg, 5 mg, 10 mg
- Tablet, extended release: 5 mg, 10 mg

## Indications

- Hypertension
- Chronic stable angina
- Chronic heart failure (stable)

## Contraindications

- Severe bronchial asthma
- Second or third degree AV block
- Cardiogenic shock
- Untreated phaeochromocytoma

## Drug Interactions

- **Verapaxil** (major): Additive AV nodal suppression, risk of bradyarrhythmia
- **Gliclazex** (moderate): Masks adrenergic warning signs of hypoglycaemia

## Use in Pregnancy and Lactation

Category C. Use only if benefit outweighs foetal risk.

## Renal Impairment

No dose adjustment for CrCl above 30 mL/min.

## Hepatic Impairment

Reduce dose in moderate to severe impairment.

## Adverse Reactions

- Bradycardia
- Fatigue
- Cold extremities
- Sleep disturbance

## Substitution Notes

Extended release form is not interchangeable milligram for milligram with the immediate
release tablet. Conversion requires prescriber review.

## Storage

Store below 25 degrees Celsius, protected from light and moisture. Keep out of
the reach of children.
