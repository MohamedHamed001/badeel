# Carvedanol

> **SYNTHETIC DATA. NOT FOR CLINICAL USE.** Every drug name, brand and clinical statement in this document is fictional and was generated for software evaluation. It does not describe any real medicine.

**ATC code:** C07AG02
**Pharmacological class:** Non-selective beta blocker with alpha-1 blockade
**Equivalence group:** `beta_blocker_nonselective`
**Narrow therapeutic index:** No

## Composition and Available Forms

- Tablet: 3.125 mg, 6.25 mg, 12.5 mg, 25 mg

## Registered Products

- Carvex (كارفكس), 6.25 mg tablet, Horus Pharm, EGP 52.0
- Carvex (كارفكس), 12.5 mg tablet, Horus Pharm, EGP 74.0
- Dilanor (ديلانور), 25 mg tablet, Nile Pharma, EGP 95.0

## Indications

- Chronic heart failure
- Hypertension

## Contraindications

- Bronchial asthma of any severity
- Decompensated heart failure
- Severe hepatic impairment
- Second or third degree AV block

## Drug Interactions

- **Gliclazex** (moderate): Enhanced hypoglycaemic effect
- **Verapaxil** (major): Severe bradycardia and hypotension

## Use in Pregnancy and Lactation

Category C.

## Renal Impairment

No adjustment required.

## Hepatic Impairment

Contraindicated in severe impairment.

## Adverse Reactions

- Postural hypotension
- Fatigue
- Weight gain

## Substitution Notes

Non-selective. Carries a stronger bronchospasm warning than the beta-1 selective agents
and is not an appropriate swap for a patient with reactive airway disease.

## Storage

Store below 25 degrees Celsius, protected from light and moisture. Keep out of
the reach of children.
