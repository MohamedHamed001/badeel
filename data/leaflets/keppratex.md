# Keppratex

> **SYNTHETIC DATA. NOT FOR CLINICAL USE.** Every drug name, brand and clinical statement in this document is fictional and was generated for software evaluation. It does not describe any real medicine.

**ATC code:** N03AX14
**Pharmacological class:** SV2A ligand antiepileptic
**Equivalence group:** `antiepileptic_broad`
**Narrow therapeutic index:** No

## Composition and Available Forms

- Tablet: 250 mg, 500 mg, 1000 mg
- Oral solution: 100 mg/mL

## Registered Products

- Levex (ليفكس), 500 mg tablet, Delta Labs, EGP 165.0
- Levex (ليفكس), 1000 mg tablet, Delta Labs, EGP 240.0

## Indications

- Focal epilepsy
- Generalised tonic clonic seizures

## Contraindications

- Known hypersensitivity

## Drug Interactions

- **Valproax** (moderate): Additive sedation

## Use in Pregnancy and Lactation

Category C. Preferred over Valproax where treatment is required.

## Renal Impairment

Dose reduction required below CrCl 50 mL/min.

## Hepatic Impairment

No adjustment for mild to moderate impairment.

## Adverse Reactions

- Somnolence
- Irritability
- Behavioural change

## Substitution Notes

Different mechanism from Valproax. Not a like for like substitution even though both are
broad spectrum.

## Storage

Store below 25 degrees Celsius, protected from light and moisture. Keep out of
the reach of children.
