> **SYNTHETIC DATA. NOT FOR CLINICAL USE.** Every drug name, brand and clinical statement in this document is fictional and was generated for software evaluation. It does not describe any real medicine.

**ATC code:** H03AA01
**Pharmacological class:** Thyroid hormone
**Equivalence group:** `thyroid_hormone`
**Narrow therapeutic index:** YES

## Composition and Available Forms

- Tablet: 25 mcg, 50 mcg, 100 mcg

## Registered Products

- Eltroxin (إلتروكسين), 50 mcg tablet, Aspen, EGP 58.0 [SHORTAGE]
- Eltroxin (إلتروكسين), 100 mcg tablet, Aspen, EGP 88.0

## Indications

- Hypothyroidism

## Contraindications

- Untreated thyrotoxicosis
- Acute myocardial infarction

## Narrow Therapeutic Index Warning

This product is designated narrow therapeutic index. Small changes in plasma concentration produce clinically significant changes in effect or toxicity. Product substitution, including substitution between brands containing the same active ingredient, must not be performed without prescriber authorisation and appropriate monitoring.

## Drug Interactions

- **Omeprazole** (moderate): Reduced absorption
- **Pantoprazole** (moderate): Reduced absorption
- **Metformin** (minor): Altered glycaemic control

## Use in Pregnancy and Lactation

Continue and monitor; requirements rise.

## Renal Impairment

No adjustment required.

## Hepatic Impairment

No adjustment required.

## Adverse Reactions

- Palpitations if over-replaced

## Substitution Notes

Narrow therapeutic index. Do not split tablets to reach a dose; retest thyroid function
after any change. Prescriber review required.

## Storage

Store below 25 degrees Celsius, protected from light and moisture. Keep out of
the reach of children.
