# Paracetamol

> **SYNTHETIC DATA. NOT FOR CLINICAL USE.** Every drug name, brand and clinical statement in this document is fictional and was generated for software evaluation. It does not describe any real medicine.

**ATC code:** N02BE01
**Pharmacological class:** Non-opioid analgesic and antipyretic
**Equivalence group:** `simple_analgesic`
**Narrow therapeutic index:** No

## Composition and Available Forms

- Tablet: 500 mg
- Oral suspension: 120 mg/5 mL

## Registered Products

- Panadol (بانادول), 500 mg tablet, GSK, EGP 30.0
- Cetal (سيتال), 500 mg tablet, EPICO, EGP 15.0
- Cetal (سيتال), 120 mg/5 mL oral suspension, EPICO, EGP 22.0

## Indications

- Pain
- Fever

## Contraindications

- Severe hepatic impairment

## Drug Interactions

- **Warfarin** (moderate): Prolonged regular use may raise INR

## Use in Pregnancy and Lactation

Analgesic of choice in pregnancy.

## Renal Impairment

No adjustment at usual doses.

## Hepatic Impairment

Reduce dose; avoid in severe impairment.

## Adverse Reactions

- Hepatotoxicity in overdose

## Substitution Notes

Not anti-inflammatory; a different pharmacological action from an NSAID and not an
equivalent substitute for inflammatory pain.

## Storage

Store below 25 degrees Celsius, protected from light and moisture. Keep out of
the reach of children.
