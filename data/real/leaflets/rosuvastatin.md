# Rosuvastatin

> **SYNTHETIC DATA. NOT FOR CLINICAL USE.** Every drug name, brand and clinical statement in this document is fictional and was generated for software evaluation. It does not describe any real medicine.

**ATC code:** C10AA07
**Pharmacological class:** HMG-CoA reductase inhibitor
**Equivalence group:** `statin`
**Narrow therapeutic index:** No

## Composition and Available Forms

- Tablet: 5 mg, 10 mg, 20 mg

## Registered Products

- Crestor (كريستور), 10 mg tablet, AstraZeneca, EGP 165.0
- Rosuvast (روزوفاست), 20 mg tablet, Marcyrl, EGP 98.0

## Indications

- Hypercholesterolaemia
- Cardiovascular risk reduction

## Contraindications

- Active liver disease
- Pregnancy
- Severe renal impairment

## Drug Interactions

- **Warfarin** (moderate): INR elevation reported

## Use in Pregnancy and Lactation

Contraindicated.

## Renal Impairment

Avoid 40 mg dose in moderate impairment.

## Hepatic Impairment

Contraindicated in active liver disease.

## Adverse Reactions

- Myalgia
- Proteinuria at high dose

## Substitution Notes

More potent per milligram than atorvastatin; requires dose conversion, not a milligram
for milligram swap.

## Storage

Store below 25 degrees Celsius, protected from light and moisture. Keep out of
the reach of children.
