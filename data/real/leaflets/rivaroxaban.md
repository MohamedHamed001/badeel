# Rivaroxaban

> **SYNTHETIC DATA. NOT FOR CLINICAL USE.** Every drug name, brand and clinical statement in this document is fictional and was generated for software evaluation. It does not describe any real medicine.

**ATC code:** B01AF01
**Pharmacological class:** Direct factor Xa inhibitor
**Equivalence group:** `doac`
**Narrow therapeutic index:** No

## Composition and Available Forms

- Tablet: 10 mg, 15 mg, 20 mg

## Registered Products

- Xarelto (زاريلتو), 20 mg tablet, Bayer, EGP 620.0

## Indications

- Atrial fibrillation
- Venous thromboembolism

## Contraindications

- Active bleeding
- Severe renal impairment
- Pregnancy

## Drug Interactions

- **Ibuprofen** (major): Increased bleeding risk
- **Ciprofloxacin** (moderate): Raised plasma concentration

## Use in Pregnancy and Lactation

Contraindicated.

## Renal Impairment

Avoid if creatinine clearance below 15 mL/min.

## Hepatic Impairment

Contraindicated in coagulopathy-associated hepatic disease.

## Adverse Reactions

- Bleeding

## Substitution Notes

A direct oral anticoagulant is not interchangeable with a vitamin K antagonist without
prescriber review.

## Storage

Store below 25 degrees Celsius, protected from light and moisture. Keep out of
the reach of children.
