# Amoxicillin

> **SYNTHETIC DATA. NOT FOR CLINICAL USE.** Every drug name, brand and clinical statement in this document is fictional and was generated for software evaluation. It does not describe any real medicine.

**ATC code:** J01CA04
**Pharmacological class:** Aminopenicillin antibiotic
**Equivalence group:** `penicillin`
**Narrow therapeutic index:** No

## Composition and Available Forms

- Capsule: 500 mg
- Oral suspension: 250 mg/5 mL

## Registered Products

- Amoxil (أموكسيل), 500 mg capsule, GSK, EGP 44.0 [SHORTAGE]
- E-Mox (إي-موكس), 500 mg capsule, EIPICO, EGP 26.0
- E-Mox (إي-موكس), 250 mg/5 mL oral suspension, EIPICO, EGP 20.0

## Indications

- Respiratory tract infection
- Otitis media
- Urinary tract infection

## Contraindications

- Penicillin hypersensitivity

## Drug Interactions

- **Warfarin** (moderate): Possible INR elevation

## Use in Pregnancy and Lactation

Considered safe.

## Renal Impairment

Reduce dose in severe impairment.

## Hepatic Impairment

Caution.

## Adverse Reactions

- Rash
- Diarrhoea

## Substitution Notes

Contraindicated in penicillin allergy; a macrolide is the usual alternative class.

## Storage

Store below 25 degrees Celsius, protected from light and moisture. Keep out of
the reach of children.
