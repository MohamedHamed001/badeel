# Bisoprolol

> **SYNTHETIC DATA. NOT FOR CLINICAL USE.** Every drug name, brand and clinical statement in this document is fictional and was generated for software evaluation. It does not describe any real medicine.

**ATC code:** C07AB07
**Pharmacological class:** Selective beta-1 adrenergic blocker
**Equivalence group:** `beta_blocker_selective`
**Narrow therapeutic index:** No

## Composition and Available Forms

- Tablet: 2.5 mg, 5 mg, 10 mg

- Bisocard (بيسوكارد), 10 mg tablet, Global Napi, EGP 92.0

## Indications

- Hypertension
- Chronic stable angina
- Chronic heart failure

## Contraindications

- Severe bronchial asthma
- Second or third degree AV block
- Cardiogenic shock
- Decompensated heart failure

## Drug Interactions

- **Verapamil** (major): Additive AV nodal suppression, risk of bradyarrhythmia
- **Gliclazide** (moderate): Masks adrenergic warning signs of hypoglycaemia

## Use in Pregnancy and Lactation

Category C. Use only if benefit outweighs foetal risk.

## Renal Impairment

No dose adjustment for eGFR above 20 mL/min.

## Hepatic Impairment

Reduce dose in severe impairment.

## Adverse Reactions

- Bradycardia
- Fatigue
- Cold extremities

## Substitution Notes

Beta-1 selective, but selectivity is relative and it is still contraindicated in severe
asthma.

## Storage

Store below 25 degrees Celsius, protected from light and moisture. Keep out of
the reach of children.
