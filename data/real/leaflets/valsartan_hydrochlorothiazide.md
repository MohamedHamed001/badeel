# Valsartan + Hydrochlorothiazide

> **SYNTHETIC DATA. NOT FOR CLINICAL USE.** Every drug name, brand and clinical statement in this document is fictional and was generated for software evaluation. It does not describe any real medicine.

**ATC code:** C09DA03
**Pharmacological class:** Angiotensin II receptor blocker with thiazide diuretic
**Equivalence group:** `raas_blocker_combination`
**Narrow therapeutic index:** No

## Composition and Available Forms

- Tablet: 80 mg / 12.5 mg, 160 mg / 12.5 mg, 160 mg / 25 mg

## Registered Products

- Co-Diovan (كو-ديوفان), 160 mg / 12.5 mg tablet, Novartis, EGP 168.0
- Co-Tareg (كو-تاريج), 80 mg / 12.5 mg tablet, Novartis, EGP 130.0

## Indications

- Hypertension not controlled on monotherapy

## Contraindications

- Pregnancy
- Anuria
- Severe hepatic impairment
- Sulfonamide hypersensitivity

## Fixed Dose Combination

This product contains Valsartan and Hydrochlorothiazide in a fixed ratio. Substitution with a product containing only one of these components leaves the other indication untreated and does not constitute therapeutic equivalence.

## Drug Interactions

- **Ibuprofen** (major): Reduced diuretic efficacy and renal risk

## Use in Pregnancy and Lactation

Contraindicated in all trimesters.

## Renal Impairment

Avoid if eGFR below 30 mL/min due to the thiazide component.

## Hepatic Impairment

Contraindicated in severe impairment.

## Adverse Reactions

- Hypokalaemia
- Hyponatraemia
- Dizziness

## Substitution Notes

A fixed dose combination; substituting a single-ingredient product leaves the second
indication untreated.

## Storage

Store below 25 degrees Celsius, protected from light and moisture. Keep out of
the reach of children.
