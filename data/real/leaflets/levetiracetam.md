# Levetiracetam

> **SYNTHETIC DATA. NOT FOR CLINICAL USE.** Every drug name, brand and clinical statement in this document is fictional and was generated for software evaluation. It does not describe any real medicine.

**ATC code:** N03AX14
**Pharmacological class:** SV2A antiepileptic
**Equivalence group:** `antiepileptic`
**Narrow therapeutic index:** No

## Composition and Available Forms

- Tablet: 250 mg, 500 mg, 1000 mg

## Registered Products

- Keppra (كيبرا), 500 mg tablet, UCB, EGP 210.0
- Tiratam (تيراتام), 500 mg tablet, Hikma, EGP 120.0

## Indications

- Focal and generalised epilepsy

## Contraindications

- Hypersensitivity to the drug

## Drug Interactions

- None recorded.

## Use in Pregnancy and Lactation

Preferred antiepileptic in pregnancy where suitable.

## Renal Impairment

Reduce dose in renal impairment.

## Hepatic Impairment

Caution in severe impairment.

## Adverse Reactions

- Somnolence
- Irritability

## Substitution Notes

Different mechanism from valproate; not an automatic substitute for seizure control
without prescriber review.

## Storage

Store below 25 degrees Celsius, protected from light and moisture. Keep out of
the reach of children.
