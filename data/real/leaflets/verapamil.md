# Verapamil

> **SYNTHETIC DATA. NOT FOR CLINICAL USE.** Every drug name, brand and clinical statement in this document is fictional and was generated for software evaluation. It does not describe any real medicine.

**ATC code:** C08DA01
**Pharmacological class:** Non-dihydropyridine calcium channel blocker
**Equivalence group:** `ccb_nondhp`
**Narrow therapeutic index:** No

## Composition and Available Forms

- Tablet: 40 mg, 80 mg
- Tablet, sustained release: 240 mg

## Registered Products

- Isoptin (إيزوبتين), 80 mg tablet, Abbott, EGP 42.0
- Isoptin SR (إيزوبتين إس آر), 240 mg tablet, sustained release, Abbott, EGP 88.0

## Indications

- Hypertension
- Angina
- Supraventricular arrhythmia

## Contraindications

- Severe left ventricular dysfunction
- Second or third degree AV block
- Concomitant beta blockade

## Drug Interactions

- **Bisoprolol** (major): Additive AV nodal suppression
- **Metoprolol** (major): Additive AV nodal suppression

## Use in Pregnancy and Lactation

Category C.

## Renal Impairment

No adjustment required.

## Hepatic Impairment

Reduce dose in impairment.

## Adverse Reactions

- Constipation
- Bradycardia
- Ankle oedema

## Substitution Notes

Sustained release and immediate release are not interchangeable milligram for milligram.

## Storage

Store below 25 degrees Celsius, protected from light and moisture. Keep out of
the reach of children.
