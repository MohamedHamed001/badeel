# Gliclazide

> **SYNTHETIC DATA. NOT FOR CLINICAL USE.** Every drug name, brand and clinical statement in this document is fictional and was generated for software evaluation. It does not describe any real medicine.

**ATC code:** A10BB09
**Pharmacological class:** Sulfonylurea antidiabetic
**Equivalence group:** `antidiabetic_oral`
**Narrow therapeutic index:** No

## Composition and Available Forms

- Tablet: 80 mg
- Tablet, modified release: 30 mg, 60 mg

## Registered Products

- Diamicron (دياميكرون), 60 mg tablet, modified release, Servier, EGP 96.0
- Diamicron (دياميكرون), 80 mg tablet, Servier, EGP 72.0

## Indications

- Type 2 diabetes mellitus

## Contraindications

- Type 1 diabetes
- Severe renal impairment
- Sulfonamide hypersensitivity

## Drug Interactions

- **Bisoprolol** (moderate): Beta blockers mask hypoglycaemia warning signs

## Use in Pregnancy and Lactation

Switch to insulin in pregnancy.

## Renal Impairment

Contraindicated in severe impairment; hypoglycaemia risk.

## Hepatic Impairment

Caution; hypoglycaemia risk.

## Adverse Reactions

- Hypoglycaemia
- Weight gain

## Substitution Notes

Carries hypoglycaemia risk in renal impairment, unlike metformin.

## Storage

Store below 25 degrees Celsius, protected from light and moisture. Keep out of
the reach of children.
