# Azithromycin

> **SYNTHETIC DATA. NOT FOR CLINICAL USE.** Every drug name, brand and clinical statement in this document is fictional and was generated for software evaluation. It does not describe any real medicine.

**ATC code:** J01FA10
**Pharmacological class:** Macrolide antibiotic
**Equivalence group:** `macrolide`
**Narrow therapeutic index:** No

## Composition and Available Forms

- Tablet: 250 mg, 500 mg
- Oral suspension: 200 mg/5 mL

## Registered Products

- Zithromax (زيثروماكس), 500 mg tablet, Pfizer, EGP 130.0
- Zisrocin (زيسروسين), 500 mg tablet, Amoun, EGP 68.0

## Indications

- Respiratory tract infection
- Atypical infection

## Contraindications

- Cholestatic jaundice with prior macrolide use

## Drug Interactions

- **Warfarin** (major): Potentiates anticoagulation
- **Ciprofloxacin** (major): Additive QT prolongation

## Use in Pregnancy and Lactation

Considered safe where indicated.

## Renal Impairment

No adjustment required.

## Hepatic Impairment

Caution.

## Adverse Reactions

- Diarrhoea
- QT prolongation

## Substitution Notes

Common alternative to a penicillin in allergic patients.

## Storage

Store below 25 degrees Celsius, protected from light and moisture. Keep out of
the reach of children.
