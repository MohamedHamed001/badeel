# Ciprofloxacin

> **SYNTHETIC DATA. NOT FOR CLINICAL USE.** Every drug name, brand and clinical statement in this document is fictional and was generated for software evaluation. It does not describe any real medicine.

**ATC code:** J01MA02
**Pharmacological class:** Fluoroquinolone antibiotic
**Equivalence group:** `fluoroquinolone`
**Narrow therapeutic index:** No

## Composition and Available Forms

- Tablet: 250 mg, 500 mg

## Registered Products

- Ciprocin (سيبروسين), 500 mg tablet, EIPICO, EGP 38.0 [SHORTAGE]
- Ciprobay (سيبروباي), 500 mg tablet, Bayer, EGP 95.0

## Indications

- Urinary tract infection
- Gastrointestinal infection

## Contraindications

- Age under 18 years except specified indications
- History of tendon disorder with quinolones
- Myasthenia gravis

## Drug Interactions

- **Azithromycin** (major): Additive QT prolongation
- **Rivaroxaban** (moderate): Raised plasma concentration
- **Metformin** (minor): Possible glycaemic fluctuation

## Use in Pregnancy and Lactation

Avoid.

## Renal Impairment

Reduce dose in impairment.

## Hepatic Impairment

Caution.

## Adverse Reactions

- Tendinopathy
- QT prolongation

## Substitution Notes

Restricted under 18 years; a paediatric prescription needs prescriber review.

## Storage

Store below 25 degrees Celsius, protected from light and moisture. Keep out of
the reach of children.
