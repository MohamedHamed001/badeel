# Valsartan

> **SYNTHETIC DATA. NOT FOR CLINICAL USE.** Every drug name, brand and clinical statement in this document is fictional and was generated for software evaluation. It does not describe any real medicine.

**ATC code:** C09CA03
**Pharmacological class:** Angiotensin II receptor blocker
**Equivalence group:** `raas_blocker`
**Narrow therapeutic index:** No

## Composition and Available Forms

- Tablet: 40 mg, 80 mg, 160 mg

## Registered Products

- Tareg (تاريج), 80 mg tablet, Novartis, EGP 96.0
- Tareg (تاريج), 160 mg tablet, Novartis, EGP 140.0
- Diovan (ديوفان), 160 mg tablet, Novartis, EGP 155.0 [SHORTAGE]

## Indications

- Hypertension
- Heart failure
- Post myocardial infarction

## Contraindications

- Pregnancy
- Bilateral renal artery stenosis
- Severe hepatic impairment

## Drug Interactions

- **Lisinopril** (major): Dual RAAS blockade
- **Ibuprofen** (major): Increased risk of acute kidney injury

## Use in Pregnancy and Lactation

Contraindicated in all trimesters.

## Renal Impairment

Monitor potassium and creatinine.

## Hepatic Impairment

Contraindicated in severe impairment.

## Adverse Reactions

- Dizziness
- Hyperkalaemia

## Substitution Notes

Does not cause the dry cough associated with ACE inhibitors, the usual switch target
from lisinopril.

## Storage

Store below 25 degrees Celsius, protected from light and moisture. Keep out of
the reach of children.
