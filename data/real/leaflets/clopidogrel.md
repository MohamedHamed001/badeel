# Clopidogrel

> **SYNTHETIC DATA. NOT FOR CLINICAL USE.** Every drug name, brand and clinical statement in this document is fictional and was generated for software evaluation. It does not describe any real medicine.

**ATC code:** B01AC04
**Pharmacological class:** P2Y12 antiplatelet
**Equivalence group:** `antiplatelet`
**Narrow therapeutic index:** No

## Composition and Available Forms

- Tablet: 75 mg

## Registered Products

- Plavix (بلافيكس), 75 mg tablet, Sanofi, EGP 190.0 [SHORTAGE]
- Clopivas (كلوبيفاس), 75 mg tablet, Marcyrl, EGP 85.0

## Indications

- Acute coronary syndrome
- Post stent
- Secondary stroke prevention

## Contraindications

- Active bleeding
- Severe hepatic impairment

## Drug Interactions

- **Omeprazole** (major): Reduced antiplatelet activation
- **Ibuprofen** (major): Increased bleeding risk

## Use in Pregnancy and Lactation

Use only if clearly needed.

## Renal Impairment

No adjustment required.

## Hepatic Impairment

Caution in impairment.

## Adverse Reactions

- Bleeding
- Bruising

## Substitution Notes

An antiplatelet is not interchangeable with an anticoagulant; they treat different
indications.

## Storage

Store below 25 degrees Celsius, protected from light and moisture. Keep out of
the reach of children.
