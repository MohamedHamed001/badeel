# Metformin

> **SYNTHETIC DATA. NOT FOR CLINICAL USE.** Every drug name, brand and clinical statement in this document is fictional and was generated for software evaluation. It does not describe any real medicine.

**ATC code:** A10BA02
**Pharmacological class:** Biguanide antidiabetic
**Equivalence group:** `antidiabetic_oral`
**Narrow therapeutic index:** No

## Composition and Available Forms

- Tablet: 500 mg, 850 mg, 1000 mg
- Tablet, extended release: 1000 mg

## Registered Products

- Glucophage (جلوكوفاج), 1000 mg tablet, Merck, EGP 68.0
- Glucophage XR (جلوكوفاج إكس آر), 1000 mg tablet, extended release, Merck, EGP 92.0 [SHORTAGE]
- Cidophage (سيدوفاج), 500 mg tablet, CID, EGP 30.0
- Cidophage (سيدوفاج), 850 mg tablet, CID, EGP 42.0

## Indications

- Type 2 diabetes mellitus

## Contraindications

- eGFR below 30 mL/min
- Acute metabolic acidosis
- Acute decompensated heart failure

## Drug Interactions

- **Ciprofloxacin** (minor): Possible glycaemic fluctuation

## Use in Pregnancy and Lactation

Compatible in pregnancy under supervision.

## Renal Impairment

Contraindicated below eGFR 30; review dose below eGFR 45.

## Hepatic Impairment

Avoid in significant impairment.

## Adverse Reactions

- Gastrointestinal upset
- Lactic acidosis (rare)

## Substitution Notes

Extended release improves gastrointestinal tolerance but changes dosing frequency; not
milligram equivalent in schedule.

## Storage

Store below 25 degrees Celsius, protected from light and moisture. Keep out of
the reach of children.
