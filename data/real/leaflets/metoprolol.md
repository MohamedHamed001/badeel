# Metoprolol

> **SYNTHETIC DATA. NOT FOR CLINICAL USE.** Every drug name, brand and clinical statement in this document is fictional and was generated for software evaluation. It does not describe any real medicine.

**ATC code:** C07AB02
**Pharmacological class:** Selective beta-1 adrenergic blocker
**Equivalence group:** `beta_blocker_selective`
**Narrow therapeutic index:** No

## Composition and Available Forms

- Tablet: 50 mg, 100 mg

## Registered Products

- Betaloc (بيتالوك), 50 mg tablet, AstraZeneca, EGP 65.0
- Betaloc (بيتالوك), 100 mg tablet, AstraZeneca, EGP 98.0

## Indications

- Hypertension
- Angina
- Migraine prophylaxis

## Contraindications

- Severe bronchial asthma
- Second or third degree AV block
- Decompensated heart failure

## Drug Interactions

- **Verapamil** (major): Additive AV nodal suppression
- **Ibuprofen** (moderate): NSAIDs may blunt antihypertensive effect

## Use in Pregnancy and Lactation

Category C.

## Renal Impairment

No adjustment required.

## Hepatic Impairment

Extensively metabolised; halve dose in cirrhosis.

## Adverse Reactions

- Bradycardia
- Dizziness
- Bronchospasm in susceptible patients

## Substitution Notes

Immediate release tablets differ from extended release; do not swap milligram for
milligram without prescriber review.

## Storage

Store below 25 degrees Celsius, protected from light and moisture. Keep out of
the reach of children.
