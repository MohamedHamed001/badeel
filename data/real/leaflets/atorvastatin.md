**Pharmacological class:** HMG-CoA reductase inhibitor
**Equivalence group:** `statin`
**Narrow therapeutic index:** No

## Indications

- Hypercholesterolaemia
- Cardiovascular risk reduction

## Contraindications

- Active liver disease
- Pregnancy
- Breastfeeding

## Drug Interactions

- **Azithromycin** (moderate): Increased myopathy risk
- **Verapamil** (moderate): Raised statin levels, myopathy risk

## Use in Pregnancy and Lactation

Contraindicated.

## Renal Impairment

No adjustment required.

## Hepatic Impairment

Contraindicated in active liver disease.

## Adverse Reactions

- Myalgia
- Deranged liver enzymes

## Substitution Notes

Potency differs across statins; a milligram for milligram swap to another statin is not
equivalent.

## Storage

Store below 25 degrees Celsius, protected from light and moisture. Keep out of
the reach of children.
