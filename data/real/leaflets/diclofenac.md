# Diclofenac

> **SYNTHETIC DATA. NOT FOR CLINICAL USE.** Every drug name, brand and clinical statement in this document is fictional and was generated for software evaluation. It does not describe any real medicine.

**ATC code:** M01AB05
**Pharmacological class:** Non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drug
**Equivalence group:** `nsaid`
**Narrow therapeutic index:** No

## Composition and Available Forms

- Tablet: 50 mg
- Gel: 1%

## Registered Products

- Cataflam (كاتافلام), 50 mg tablet, Novartis, EGP 40.0
- Voltaren (فولتارين), 50 mg tablet, Novartis, EGP 45.0 [SHORTAGE]
- Olfen Gel (أولفين جيل), 1% gel, Mepha, EGP 55.0

## Indications

- Pain
- Inflammation
- Musculoskeletal disorders

## Contraindications

- Active peptic ulcer
- Established ischaemic heart disease
- Severe renal impairment
- Third trimester pregnancy

## Drug Interactions

- **Warfarin** (major): Increased bleeding risk
- **Lisinopril** (major): Acute kidney injury risk

## Use in Pregnancy and Lactation

Avoid, especially third trimester.

## Renal Impairment

Avoid in significant impairment.

## Hepatic Impairment

Caution.

## Adverse Reactions

- Dyspepsia
- Gastrointestinal bleeding
- Raised blood pressure

## Substitution Notes

Carries a higher cardiovascular risk than some other NSAIDs; avoid in established
ischaemic heart disease.

## Storage

Store below 25 degrees Celsius, protected from light and moisture. Keep out of
the reach of children.
